Privacy Policy

TinyTask Lab is designed for browser-first utility work. This policy explains the limited data we handle.

Your tool inputs

Tool inputs and files are processed in your browser unless a tool clearly says otherwise. We do not ask you to create an account to use the free tools, and we do not receive the text, images, or files you process locally.

Device-local information

We use browser storage to remember local tool usage, your analytics preference, and, after a verified Pro purchase, a device-specific access token. Clearing browser storage may remove these settings and local Pro access on that device.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal. TinyTask Lab does not store card numbers, PayPal passwords, or funding-source credentials. We store only the order reference, payment status, amount, currency, and capture reference needed to verify access, handle refunds, and maintain transaction records.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics only after you choose “Allow analytics.” Analytics storage is denied by default, advertising storage and personalization remain denied, and you can decline without losing access to the tools. We use visit and feature-use events to understand service health and usage trends. We do not include tool inputs, uploaded files, filenames, clipboard contents, payment details, or account identifiers in those events.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@tinytasklab.com.
