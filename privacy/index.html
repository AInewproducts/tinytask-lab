Privacy Policy

TinyTask Lab is designed for browser-first utility work. This policy explains the limited data we handle.

Your tool inputs

Tool inputs and files are processed in your browser unless a tool clearly says otherwise. We do not ask you to create an account to use the free tools, and we do not receive the text, images, or files you process locally.

Usage measurement

This public site does not load third-party behavioral analytics and does not currently send usage events. Before any measurement is enabled, TinyTask Lab will use only a first-party endpoint that accepts the minimum service-health events: page view, tool start, tool success, download, upgrade click, and error. It will not accept tool inputs, files, filenames, clipboard contents, URLs, referrers, account identifiers, or stable device identifiers.

Device-local information

We use browser storage to remember local tool usage and, after a verified Pro purchase, a device-specific access token. Clearing browser storage may remove these settings and local Pro access on that device.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayPal. TinyTask Lab does not store card numbers, PayPal passwords, or funding-source credentials. We store only the order reference, payment status, amount, currency, and capture reference needed to verify access, handle refunds, and maintain transaction records.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@tinytasklab.com.
